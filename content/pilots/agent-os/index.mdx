# Agent OS

An Agent OS is a personal AI operating system built on Claude Code. Not a chatbot. Not a note-taking app. An operating layer that knows your business, reaches your tools, executes workflows, and runs scheduled work while you sleep.

The litmus test: while you are not at your desk, your Agent OS observes a real-world event and produces an output that is faster and more accurate than what you would produce yourself. That is what done looks like.

## The four layers

An Agent OS is built in four layers, in order. Each layer depends on the one before it.

**Context** -- Claude knows your business. A fresh session can answer "what does this business do, who are the customers, and what are the active projects?" without you explaining anything. This lives in `CLAUDE.md` and a `context/` folder in your working directory.

**Connections** -- Claude can reach your tools. Calendar, email, Slack, n8n, your Obsidian vault, your CRM. A `connections.md` registry maps every system the OS can touch, with the auth and invocation pattern for each.

**Capabilities** -- Claude knows how to do the work. A short phrase or slash command triggers a multi-step workflow that produces a real artifact: a morning brief, a captured and classified note, a drafted reply, a weekly review. vault-intake is the first Capability in this pilot.

**Cadence** -- the OS runs without being asked. Scheduled `claude -p` commands via cron or n8n fire while your laptop is closed. Overnight work lands in your vault or inbox. A morning brief is waiting when you open your computer. The mini-PC running headless Ubuntu is where this layer lives permanently.

Do not build Cadence before the first three layers work manually. Automating a broken workflow produces broken output on a schedule.

## What done looks like

You open your laptop in the morning. In your vault: three new notes captured and classified overnight from sources your n8n pipeline was watching. A morning brief synthesized from the week's captures, with two connection alerts between notes you wrote months apart and one suggested action. A draft reply to the email that came in at 2am, staged for your review.

You did not touch a keyboard. Your Agent OS ran the Cadence layer while you slept.

That is the end state. Build 1 of this pilot is the first Capability: vault-intake. It is the piece that writes structured, classified, wikilinked notes into your vault from any text source. Without it, there is nothing for the synthesis layer to work with and nothing for the Cadence layer to automate.

## The vault portability principle

Everything vault-intake writes is plain Markdown with YAML frontmatter. No proprietary format, no sync service, no vendor lock-in. If you stop using vault-intake tomorrow, every note it wrote is still readable, searchable, and linkable in Obsidian. The Agent OS extends your vault. It does not own it.

AI tools that own your knowledge create dependency. Tools that write to your vault extend it.
